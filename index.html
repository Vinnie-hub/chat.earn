Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about Chat&Earn

How do payments work?
Payments are processed weekly every Friday. You can withdraw your earnings once you reach $20 minimum via PayPal, bank transfer, or mobile money. There are no fees for withdrawals.
What are the requirements to join?
You need to be 18+ years old, have a stable internet connection, and pay the $10 account activation fee. No specific education or experience is required.
What topics can I chat about?
You can discuss hobbies, travel, culture, life experiences, advice, and general interests. All conversations must be respectful and appropriate. We provide topic suggestions to help you get started.
How much time do I need to commit?
You can chat as little or as much as you want. Some members chat 1-2 hours daily ($200-400/month), while others chat 4+ hours daily ($800-1500/month). It's completely flexible.
