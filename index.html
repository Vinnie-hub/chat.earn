It depends on your effort. You earn a flat $3 per completed chat. Our calculator shows the math: 5 chats/day ≈ $450/month. 10 chats/day ≈ $900/month.

Have friendly conversations with real people — about culture, travel, life, advice, or hobbies. No experience needed. Just be yourself and chat like you would with a friend.

Just a smartphone and an internet connection. No laptop or special gear required. You can do it from anywhere.

As little or as much as you want. Even 30 minutes a day earns real cash. Students, parents, and full-time workers all use this in their free time.

Zero hidden fees or monthly subscriptions after activation — you pay once, that's it.
